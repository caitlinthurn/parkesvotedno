Your bin, burned around the clock beside the farms that feed you.

An investment consortium wants to burn 700,000 tonnes of Sydney’s rubbish a year in NSW grain country. There is no plan to test the food grown there. Plants like this are not allowed in Sydney, yet a farming town is expected to live beside one so Sydney doesn’t have to.

Parkes Shire Council voted unanimously against it.

Objections close on Thursday 17 December 2026.

Lodge an objection in ten minutes

Anyone can object, wherever they live. Each person has to lodge their own.

  1. Add your details

    The Department groups identical letters together. A sentence of your own makes yours count as a separate objection.

    Have you made a political donation of $1,000 or more in the last two years?

    The Department requires every submission to declare this. Your answer goes into the letter. If you answer yes, the portal will also ask you for the details when you lodge.

  2. Copy your letter

  3. Open the NSW Planning Portal

    Press “Make a submission” on the project page. You will be asked to create a free account with your own email address.

    Open the project page

    If the button does not open, copy this address into your browser: https://www.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/major-projects/projects/parkes-energy-recovery-facility

  4. Paste and submit

    Choose “Object”. Paste the letter into the comments box. Answer the portal’s political donations question the same way you answered it here. Tick the declaration and submit.

    Submissions are published on the portal. You can ask for your name to be withheld. If you do, delete your name and suburb from the bottom of the letter before you submit.

What you are signing

Every claim in the letter comes from the company’s own documents on the portal, or from the sources below. Read these before you lodge.

The seven points and where they come from
  • Breakdowns were not modelled. The government’s checklist for the project (the SEARs) required it. The air quality report (Appendix Q), page xvi and section 9, says it was not done.
  • The project has been split up. The Chullora transfer station is a separate application, SSD-133259740, that has not been exhibited. The Parkes rail terminal is under another approval. The main EIS report, section 18.5.6, names Kemps Creek and Lucas Heights as possible landfills for the filter residue.
  • The technology is not proven to NSW standards. Air quality report (Appendix Q), executive summary. The reference plant at Ardley in England burns 378,000 tonnes a year.
  • The need depends on recycling not improving. The waste flow analysis (Appendix L), executive summary and sections 2 and 3.
  • Food and farmland. The farm study (Appendix S). The health study (Appendix R), page 23. The GrainCorp Grower Warehousing Agreement, clause 9 and Part C.
  • Costs fall on Parkes. Water figure from the main EIS report, section 3.4.14. The council vote was on 17 February 2026.
  • Fairness. The four permitted precincts are listed in the main EIS report, section 2.6.1.
